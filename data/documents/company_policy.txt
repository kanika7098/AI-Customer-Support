E-COMMERCE CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

RETURN POLICY

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Some products may not be eligible for return, including personalized products and certain hygiene-related products.

REFUND POLICY

After a returned product is inspected and approved, the refund is initiated.

Refunds are normally processed within 5-7 business days.

The actual time for the money to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider.

ORDER CANCELLATION

Customers can request cancellation before an order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible. The customer may instead need to use the return process after delivery.

DELIVERY POLICY

Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days after shipment.

Delivery times can vary depending on location, courier delays, weather, and other circumstances.

If an order is significantly delayed, the customer should contact customer support with their order number.

DAMAGED PRODUCTS

Customers who receive a damaged product should contact customer support as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs of the damaged product and its packaging.

The support team will review the case and determine the appropriate resolution.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers should provide their order number when contacting support about an existing order.

The AI support agent must not invent order status, refund status, delivery status, or other information that is not available in the company's systems.

If the AI cannot confidently answer a question using the available company information, the issue should be escalated to a human support agent.
